Experience

THE PLATFORM
IN ACTION

Built for clarity at every step. Here's what it looks like when owners upload, builders discover, and decisions get made.

01 — Project Upload

One project page. Everything a builder needs.

Add your project name, type, location and package — then attach your plans, scope and specs. Done. No follow-up emails. No re-sending files.

  • Structured project details in a single form
  • Drag-and-drop document upload
  • Your project goes live instantly to matched builders
  • Edit or update at any time
02 — Builder Discovery

Real residential work. No chasing required.

Builders see a live feed of projects matched to their location, trade type and capacity. Every listing is a real opportunity — not a cold lead.

  • Filtered by location, type and project size
  • Unlock full documents before committing to a tender
  • See tender deadline and competition level upfront
  • Save projects for later review
03 — Compare & Decide

See every tender. Make a considered choice.

All builder responses land in one place. Review credentials, pricing and tender detail side by side — then move forward with the right builder, not just the first available one.

  • Side-by-side builder and tender review
  • Full builder profiles and credentials visible
  • Ask questions directly from the comparison view
  • Mark builders as shortlisted or declined
04 — Messaging

Conversations stay close to the project.

Ask questions, clarify scope and progress discussions — all within the project itself. No separate email threads, no lost context, no miscommunication.

  • Project-level messaging with any builder
  • Questions and answers visible to relevant parties
  • Full history tied to each project
  • Notifications when builders respond
