01 — Project Feed

A live feed of work that actually fits.

Verified residential projects, filtered to your trade, area, scale and capacity. No cold-call lists. No commercial. No noise. Just real opportunities, the moment they go live.

  • Filter by location, build type and project size
  • See deadlines and competition level upfront

02 — Unlock Project

Full plans, scope and specs — before you commit.

One click unlocks the entire project pack. Read every plan, scope note and spec sheet right where the project lives — so you know whether to estimate before you spend a minute on it.

Tender direct to the owner. No middleman.

Submit a structured tender — your numbers, your scope, your story — directly to the project owner. They review you alongside other shortlisted builders, in the same view as your full profile.

  • Structured tender format owners can compare fairly
  • Your profile, licence and track record visible
  • Ask scope questions inside the project
  • No reseller, no shared lead, no fee per submission
04 — Pipeline

Every opportunity, every conversation, in one place.

A clean pipeline view of every project you've unlocked, every tender you've sent and every conversation in flight. No more juggling email threads, spreadsheets and missed follow-ups.

  • Pipeline status: unlocked · tendering · submitted · won
