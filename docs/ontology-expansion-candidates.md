# EXPANSION CANDIDATES — Scope Standard v1.2.0 → v1.3 proposal (66 items)

## preliminaries
1. **preliminaries.site-security-monitoring** — Site security: alarm/CCTV/camera hire and after-hours monitoring for the duration. CONDITIONAL — theft-risk clause in prelims, inner-urban multi site, or specified site cameras.
2. **preliminaries.overhead-gantry-hoarding** — A-class/B-class hoarding with overhead gantry protecting public footpath during boundary construction. CONDITIONAL — zero-lot boundary wall over a public way; council hoarding permit in DA conditions. (Distinct from temporary-fencing, whose "hoarding" alias means fence-line hoarding.)
3. **preliminaries.statutory-levies** — Statutory construction levies: long service leave levy, building services levy, ACT/state training levies. CORE — payable on every project above the state threshold; contract sum evidences it.
4. **preliminaries.construction-management-plan** — Preparing and implementing a council-required construction/environmental management plan (CMP/CEMP). CONDITIONAL — DA condition requiring CMP; multi-dwelling or constrained-access site.
5. **preliminaries.service-locating** — Locating and potholing existing underground services before excavation (DBYD follow-up, vacuum potholing). CONDITIONAL — KDR/renovation, or services shown "location unverified" on civil drawings.
6. **preliminaries.materials-testing** — Independent testing: concrete cylinders/slump, fill compaction (Level 1), soil density certificates. CONDITIONAL — engineer's spec calls for tested/controlled fill or concrete testing.
7. **preliminaries.vibration-monitoring** — Vibration and movement monitoring of adjoining structures during rock breaking, piling or demolition. CONDITIONAL — rock excavation or piling within influence of neighbouring buildings; protection-works determination requiring monitoring.
8. **preliminaries.access-oversail-licences** — Negotiating and paying for neighbour access licences and crane oversail/airspace agreements. CONDITIONAL — scaffold/crane shown over adjoining land on the site plan; boundary-to-boundary footprint.
9. **preliminaries.defects-liability-attendance** — Attendance, rectification and re-inspection during the defects liability period, including handover walk-through. COMMERCIAL — contract names a DLP; multi-dwelling with staged settlements.

## approvals
10. **approvals.strata-subdivision** — Strata/community title subdivision: survey plans, unit entitlements, plan registration and owners-corporation establishment. CONDITIONAL — multi-dwelling/duplex with separate titles noted on DA or contract.
11. **approvals.heritage-approvals** — Heritage permits, heritage-advisor review and any required conservation management documentation. CONDITIONAL — heritage overlay/listing on planning certificate; heritage conditions on DA.
12. **approvals.flood-overlay-compliance** — Flood-prone land compliance: flood report, minimum floor level certification, flood-resilient construction requirements. CONDITIONAL — flood overlay on planning certificate; FFL relative to flood planning level on drawings.

## demolition
13. **demolition.lead-hazmat-removal** — Removing non-asbestos hazardous materials: lead paint, synthetic mineral fibre, PCB fittings, with clearance testing. CONDITIONAL — hazmat survey identifying lead/SMF; pre-1970 dwelling strip-out.
14. **demolition.underground-tank-removal** — Decommissioning and removing buried tanks: septic, fuel or disused rainwater tanks, with validation of the void. CONDITIONAL — KDR on unsewered-history lot; tank shown on survey or disclosed by owner.
15. **demolition.pool-removal-backfill** — Demolishing an existing pool, breaking the shell for drainage and backfilling with certified compacted fill. CONDITIONAL — existing pool on survey with new works over or near its footprint; KDR.
16. **demolition.chimney-removal** — Removing a redundant chimney and stack, making good roof, floor and wall openings. CONDITIONAL — renovation drawings showing chimney deleted; demolition plan hatching a fireplace.

## alterations
17. **alterations.structural-crack-repair** — Stitching, epoxy injection and rebuilding of cracked structural masonry before new work loads it. CONDITIONAL — engineer's report noting existing cracking/movement; dilapidation findings.
18. **alterations.damp-remediation** — Retrofitting damp-proof courses and treating rising damp in retained walls. CONDITIONAL — reno/extension with salt attack or damp noted in inspection reports; heritage masonry retained.
19. **alterations.house-raising** — Jacking and raising the existing dwelling onto new supports to build under or reset floor levels. CONDITIONAL — "raise and build under" on drawings; QLD-style raised-dwelling sections. (Distinct from footings-slab.restumping, which is like-for-like re-support.)
20. **alterations.facade-retention** — Temporarily bracing and permanently retaining an existing facade while the structure behind is rebuilt. CONDITIONAL — heritage street frontage retained on demolition plan; facade-retention engineering.

## site-services
21. **site-services.stormwater-pump-out** — Pumped stormwater: sump, pumps and rising main where levels cannot drain by gravity, including basement seepage sumps. CONDITIONAL — basement/undercroft below street invert; charged or pump-out system on hydraulic drawings. (sewer-pump covers sewage only.)
22. **site-services.power-upgrade-augmentation** — Network augmentation for larger loads: consumer mains upgrade, new pillar/pit, substation or transformer contribution. CONDITIONAL — multi-dwelling load assessment; three-phase or all-electric large dwelling with network application noted.
23. **site-services.recycled-water-connection** — Connecting to dual-reticulation recycled water (purple pipe) for toilets and irrigation where the estate provides it. CONDITIONAL — recycled water main shown in estate engineering; developer guidelines mandate connection.
24. **site-services.greywater-recycling** — Greywater diversion or treatment system reusing laundry/bathroom water on gardens. CONDITIONAL — sustainability report or owner brief nominating greywater reuse.
25. **site-services.bore-water-supply** — Bore, bore pump and header supply for irrigation or non-potable use. CONDITIONAL — rural/regional lot with bore nominated on site plan or owner brief.
26. **site-services.water-sub-metering** — Individual water check-meters and manifolds per dwelling for strata billing. CONDITIONAL — multi-dwelling; water authority sub-metering requirement on hydraulic drawings. (electrical.separate-metering covers power only.)
27. **site-services.wsud-raingarden** — Water-sensitive urban design assets: raingardens, bioretention beds and their overflow connections required by the consent. CONDITIONAL — WSUD/stormwater quality condition on DA; raingarden detail on civil drawings.
28. **site-services.sewer-encasement-buildover** — Protecting authority sewers built over or near: concrete encasement, piering to angle of repose, authority inspections. CONDITIONAL — build-over-easement/asset consent; sewer main within the footprint's zone of influence on the service diagram.
