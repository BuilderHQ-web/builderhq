01 Principles

People come to BuilderHQ to upload their plans and choose who builds their house. It is one of the largest financial decisions of their lives. Everything in this book exists to earn that.

BuilderHQ is a light brand.

Warm off-white canvas, ink text, teal accents. The marketing site and the signed-in application run on one identity. Any creative built on a dark navy ground is off-brand. A deep navy is retained as a single token for deliberate high-contrast moments, the email shell and occasional editorial bands. It is an accent surface, never the ground.

The character

Calm, warm, editorial, precise. Generous whitespace. Hairline rules rather than boxes. The reference points are a considered architectural monograph or a serious financial publication, not a construction website and not a startup SaaS page.

Three rules that outrank taste

  • Teal is a fill, never type on light. See section 03. This one is a contrast failure, not a preference.
  • No em dashes in any rendered copy. Commas, colons or full stops.
  • Australian English throughout. Organise, colour, licence as a noun, license as a verb, programme for a build schedule.

03 The teal rule

Teal #00d4c8 is a fill colour. It is not a type colour.

As text on the warm canvas it measures 1.76:1, well under the 4.5:1 WCAG AA minimum for body copy. When teal needs to be text, use #0a7d73. Bright teal type is legal only on the retained navy.

Correct

Request an introduction

Teal as a fill, dark ink on top.

Teal as type, at #0a7d73.

Never

Teal type at #00d4c8 on cream.

Unreadable at 1.76:1. This exact line is the failure the rule exists to prevent.

Never place the raw PNG and set a height.

The visible glyph occupies a 327 × 80 strip inside a 500 × 500 canvas, offset 28.5px left of centre. Rendering it directly leaves enormous padding and a tiny mark. Use the <Logo> component, which scales and offsets the bitmap so the glyph sits exactly in its box. Pass tone="dark" on light grounds.

08 Elevation

Elevation is the single move that turns a flat rectangle on the warm canvas into a surface sitting above the page. A tight contact shadow for the edge, plus a soft wide ambient drop. Never a heavy halo.

On the canvas, so the lift is visible
card-elev
card-elev-lg
no shadow
  • card-elev is the default for any card.
  • card-elev-lg is the hover and raised state, and the treatment for genuinely prominent panels.
  • hairline is a gradient rule that fades at both ends, for separation inside a card that should barely register.
  • glass and glass-strong exist for floating chrome: translucent white with a 20 to 28px backdrop blur.

09 Motion

The house easing is a premium ease-out. Movement is short, confident and never bouncy except where a spring is explicitly wanted.

TokenCurveUse
ease-outcubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)The default for everything
ease-out-softcubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)Longer, gentler entrances
ease-in-out-softcubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)Two-way transitions
ease-springcubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)Deliberate overshoot only

Durations

  • 160ms form fields and small state changes.
  • 240ms buttons and hover. Slow enough never to flicker.
  • 600 to 900ms entrance reveals: fade-in, rise-in, which lifts 8px while fading.

Reduced motion is honoured globally.

Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation and transition collapses to 0.001ms and smooth scrolling is disabled. Never re-enable motion locally.

10 Components

Buttons

Eight variants. Primary is a flat plate of brand colour: no gradient, no border, no shadow.

Variants
Sizes · sm and md are 8px radius, lg and xl are pills
  • Default is variant="primary" size="md", sentence case.
  • Active compresses to scale(0.985). It never resizes.
  • Disabled drops to 40% opacity and removes pointer events.
  • Loading swaps the leading icon for a spinner and sets aria-busy.
  • Pill sizes are for hero CTAs and focused screens: auth, onboarding, upload.

Form fields

Quiet by default. Flat surface, hairline border, 3px radius. Focus is a small teal halo only, no inner gradient and no outer glow, so a form reads as precise rather than decorated.

Click the field to see the focus treatment
  • Focus: border to rgba(9,148,137,0.5) plus a 3px rgba(0,212,200,0.15) halo.
  • Invalid: border to rgba(255,80,80,0.50), halo shifts red.
  • Below 1024px all controls are forced to 16px so iOS does not zoom on tap.

Cards

Surface 1 fill, hairline border, card-elev, radius between 8 and 12px. Never a heavier border to create separation. If a card needs more presence, raise the elevation.

11 Focus and accessibility

Keyboard focus is a two-ring treatment: a 1px ring in the canvas colour to separate the control from its surroundings, then a 3px teal ring at 55% alpha. Applied globally via :focus-visible, so it follows the keyboard and never appears on a mouse click.

Tab to this button to see it
  • Text selection is teal at 32% alpha with near-black text.
  • Scrollbars are 10px, ink at low alpha, thumb only, no track.
  • On touch devices the blue tap flash is removed, because every control has a real :active state.
  • Body copy must clear 4.5:1. This is why the teal rule in section 03 exists.

The hard rules

  • Inline styles only. Clients strip style blocks from the body.
  • Never roll your own button. Use the shell's PrimaryButton, which emits VML for Outlook.
  • 560px container, collapsing to full width at 480px.
  • Every footer carries the physical address, support email and a "why am I getting this" line. That is an Australian Spam Act 2003 requirement and the strongest deliverability signal after SPF and DKIM.
  • Compose from the shell primitives, never raw sections with inline styles.

14 Voice

The benchmark is a council, realestate.com.au, the HIA. Organisations nobody questions the seriousness of. Credible and institutional, but not dated.

The rules

  • Australian English throughout.
  • No em dashes anywhere in copy. Ads, emails, captions, everything.
  • No slang, no flippancy. Never: ghosted, ghost jobs, tyre-kickers, bidding circus, no catch, no worries, ballpark, move the needle, playing telephone, bleed, you're the product, or any infomercial phrasing.
  • Measured and confident, never salesy. Value-forward statements, not anxious questions. No exclamation marks, no manufactured urgency.
  • Truthful and precise. An ACCC requirement as well as a house rule. The address stays private until a verified builder secures a tender spot, not "until you choose who to talk to".
  • Natural over formal. "Key details", not "Project fact sheet".
  • Plain, precise, human. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Specifics beat adjectives.

The register to match

"Every builder prices that same scope, so you can see what sits behind each number."

"The cheapest quote is usually the one that left the most out. The rest arrives later, at a price nobody competed on."

"We read the client's documents and lay out every item, so you price instead of investigating."

"Checked by people, not just code."

15 Imagery

  • Real architectural documents, drawings, plans and product interface are on-brand, and are the strongest assets we have.
  • Real project photography only with permission and credit. We never republish third-party photography.
  • Eleven commissioned ink illustrations of house types serve as project covers.
  • No generic construction stock. Hard hats, high-vis, thumbs up, handshakes on site. It undermines the premium positioning immediately.

Partner asset convention

  • Work photography: 1254 × 1254 JPEG, quality 86, progressive.
  • Logos: square PNG on pure white. Off-white shows as a visible square inside the avatar tile.
  • Trim a logo to its own bounds and centre it with an even margin, so it fills the tile.
  • Path: /public/partners/<slug>/.

16 Known drift

Three places where the codebase disagrees with this book. Recorded rather than tidied away, because a brand book that hides its own exceptions stops being trustworthy.

1. src/lib/tokens.ts is stale, and claims not to be.

Its header says it is the single source of truth and mirrors globals.css. It does not. It still describes the retired navy identity: #03090f canvas, cool-blue borders, #eef6ff text. Nothing imports it, so it is dead code, but it is dangerous dead code: it is the first file a new developer would trust. It should be deleted or regenerated from globals.css.

2. The email shell declares a Bebas Neue display stack.

Bebas Neue belongs to the retired identity and is not loaded anywhere, so the heading falls through to Helvetica or Impact depending on the client. Emails are otherwise correct. The stack should resolve to the same system sans as the body.

3. Instrument Serif is loaded globally for narrow use.

It is fetched on every page but used only in the Build Brief, Owner Advisory and pull quotes. Not a correctness problem, but worth scoping if page weight matters.

Maintaining this book

src/app/globals.css is the source of truth for every colour, radius, easing and utility. If a value changes there, change it here. If this book and the code disagree, the code is what customers see, and this book is wrong until it is fixed.
